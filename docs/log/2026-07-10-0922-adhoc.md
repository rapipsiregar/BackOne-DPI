## 2. Steps Taken
1. **Identified the Root Cause**:
   - The flat `Math.min(0.2, ...)` cap on `arcAltitude` forced long-distance curves (such as Jakarta to Brazil/Peru) to have a peak altitude lower than the chord depth of the sphere.
   - Consequently, the arc lines pierced through the sphere's interior, submerging under the globe surface. This hidden rendering created the illusion that the labels had no flow lines.
2. **Mathematically Derived a Great-Circle Altitude Formula**:
   - Calculated the angular great-circle distance $\theta$ in radians between start and end coordinates.
   - Calculated the chord depth inside a unit sphere: `chordDepth = 1 - Math.cos(theta / 2)`.
   - Set the `arcAltitude` dynamically to `chordDepth + 0.05 + theta * 0.03`. This ensures the curve always clears the sphere surface beautifully, hovering just above the terrain, without shooting too far off-canvas.
